<section id="scientific-boundary" class="section product-boundary-section">
  <div class="section-heading wide-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">Scientific boundary</p>
    <h2>An audit framework, not an automatic quality score.</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="boundary-grid">
    <article class="boundary-card boundary-does">
      <span class="boundary-label">GazeAudit does</span>
      <h3>Make methodological choices explicit.</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Surface structural QC diagnostics.</li>
        <li>Propagate declared measurement uncertainty.</li>
        <li>Evaluate researcher-defined specification spaces.</li>
        <li>Record readiness and publication provenance.</li>
      </ul>
    </article>
    <article class="boundary-card boundary-does-not">
      <span class="boundary-label">GazeAudit does not</span>
      <h3>Decide scientific validity for you.</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>It does not provide universal QC cutoffs.</li>
        <li>It does not automatically exclude participants.</li>
        <li>It does not search for a favourable specification.</li>
        <li>It does not turn sensitivity summaries into causal evidence.</li>
      </ul>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
